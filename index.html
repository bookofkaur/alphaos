Options academy

Core mechanics that determine profit and loss.

Call

The right to buy 100 shares at the strike. Long calls are generally bullish. Maximum loss is the premium paid.

Put

The right to sell 100 shares at the strike. Long puts are generally bearish. Maximum loss is the premium paid.

Premium

A $2.50 option normally costs $250 because one standard equity contract represents 100 shares.

Delta

Directional exposure and estimated option-price change for a $1 stock move.

Theta

Estimated loss from time decay, all else equal.

Vega

Sensitivity to implied volatility. IV crush can hurt even when direction is correct.
Beginner default: defined risk, liquid contracts, sufficient time, and limit orders.
